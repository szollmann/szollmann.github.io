Visual Computing Aarhus

Visual Computing Aarhus (VCA) is a research group in the Human-Centered Computing Section at the Department of Computer Science, Aarhus University. Led by Associate Professor Stefanie Zollmann, our work lies at the intersection of computer graphics, computer vision, human-computer interaction (HCI), and visualization.

We investigate how visual computing can connect real and virtual environments and support human perception, interaction, and understanding. Our research includes eXtended Reality (XR), Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), situated visualization, immersive rendering, view synthesis, and methods for creating spatial and immersive content.

Meet the team ↓

Research

  • Situated Visualization and Augmented Reality

    We develop methods for presenting digital information and visualizations directly in their real-world spatial context.

  • XR for Sports, Media, Training, and Instruction

    We investigate augmented and virtual reality for sports spectating, immersive coaching, training, and situated instruction.

  • Immersive Rendering and View Synthesis

    We explore neural rendering, Gaussian splatting, panoramic reconstruction, novel-view synthesis, and view-dependent rendering for interactive XR.

  • Computer Vision for Spatial Computing

    We study camera-based localization, tracking, reconstruction, and scene understanding for augmented and mixed reality systems.
